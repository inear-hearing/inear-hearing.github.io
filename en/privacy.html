Privacy Policy

Guiin Hearing Aid Co., Ltd. (“Guiin Hearing”) values your personal information and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws.

1. Personal information we collect

This website does not offer member registration or bulletin boards and does not use cookie-based tracking; simply browsing the site collects no personal information. During phone consultations or appointment booking, you may provide your name, contact number, and information about your hearing.

2. Purpose of use

Information provided during phone consultations is used only for consultation, appointment guidance, and after-care (A/S) contact. It is never used for any other purpose or shared with third parties.

3. Retention and disposal

Personal information is retained only until the purpose of consultation and after-care is fulfilled, and is promptly destroyed afterwards or upon your request.

4. External service links

When you use external service links on this site, such as Naver Map or Kakao Map, the privacy policy of the respective service applies.
