From Yongwon or Ungdong to Guiin Hearing Jinhae Center — address, parking, bus stop

Guiin Hearing · Written 2026-10-24 · Center information

Key points
  • Jinhae Center: 1F Unit 108, Hi-Zone Bldg., 999 Jinhae-daero, Jinhae-gu, Changwon (Jaeun-dong).
  • By car: underground parking at the Hi-Zone shopping arcade (parking supported).
  • By bus: Jaeun-bondong stop (자은본동, No. 518139, towards Jaeun POSCO) — routes 155, 156, 164, 307, 315, 317, 3006.
  • If Gangseo, Busan is closer, visit the Myeongji Service Center (Bldg. A Unit 1070, 99 Myeongji-gukje 6-ro, Gangseo-gu, Busan). Please book before visiting.

Is there a hearing aid center near Ungdong or Yongwon?

  • Guiin Hearing Jinhae Center (main) — 1F Unit 108, Hi-Zone Bldg., 999 Jinhae-daero, Jinhae-gu, Changwon (Jaeun-dong)
  • Guiin Hearing Myeongji Service Center — Bldg. A Unit 1070, Busan Myeongji Daebang DM City Centum Ocean 1st, 99 Myeongji-gukje 6-ro, Gangseo-gu, Busan
  • Phone 010-6599-0109 / 0507-1426-0116 (both centers) · please book before visiting.

Where can I park?

  • Enter “진해대로 999” or “하이존빌딩” in your navigation app.
  • Use the underground parking at the Hi-Zone shopping arcade (parking supported).
  • For the Myeongji Service Center, use the Daebang DM City arcade parking (parking supported).

Which bus stop is closest?

  • Nearest stop: Jaeun-bondong (자은본동), stop No. 518139, towards Jaeun POSCO
  • Routes stopping here: 155, 156, 164, 307, 315, 317, 3006
  • Routes, transfers and travel time from Yongwon or Ungdong vary by starting point and time. Search with Naver Map, Kakao Map or the Changwon bus information service using “자은본동” as the destination.

Bus routes and schedules can change. Check a map app or the Changwon bus information service before you go.

Q. Is there a hearing aid center near Ungdong or Yongwon?

A. Guiin Hearing Jinhae Center (1F Unit 108, Hi-Zone Bldg., 999 Jinhae-daero, Jaeun-dong, Jinhae-gu, Changwon). If Gangseo, Busan is closer, there is also the Myeongji Service Center. Call 010-6599-0109 to book.

Q. Is there parking at the Jinhae Center?

A. Yes, the underground parking at the Hi-Zone shopping arcade (parking supported). The nearest bus stop is Jaeun-bondong (No. 518139).

Bus routes and schedules can change. Check a map app or the Changwon bus information service before you go.
