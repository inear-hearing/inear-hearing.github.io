The 6-Month Prescription Deadline for Hearing Aids: How to Count the Dates

Guiin Hearing · Written 2026-10-23 · Program details based on the NHIS website, October 2026

Key points
  • NHIS rule: only assistive devices bought within 6 months of the prescription issue date are covered.
  • The date that counts is the issue date printed on the prescription — not the day you visited the clinic or our center.
  • Tip: set your own deadline at issue date + 5 months to leave a month of margin.
  • After purchase: inspection after 1 month of use → claim → follow-up fitting every year from 1 year after purchase. Service life is 5 years.

In subsidy consultations, the most common problem is not the paperwork — it is the date.

After getting a prescription, life gets busy and months pass quickly. But Korea's National Health Insurance hearing aid benefit has a fixed deadline. Here is how to mark it on your calendar.

The NHIS rule: "within 6 months of the prescription issue date"

The National Health Insurance Service (NHIS, 국민건강보험공단) claim guidance for hearing aids says:

"Only assistive devices purchased within 6 months of the prescription issue date are eligible for the benefit." (처방전 발행일로부터 6개월 이내 구입한 보조기기만 급여 가능)

The date that counts is the day the prescription was issued — not the day you visited the clinic or the day of your first consultation with us. Please check the issue date printed on the prescription (처방전).

How to count the dates

On the day you receive the prescription, mark three dates on your calendar.

  1. Issue date — copy the date printed on the prescription.
  2. The 6-month date — mark the date six months later. Example: issued March 10 → around September 10.
  3. Your own deadline (recommended: issue date + 5 months) — mark a date one month earlier to leave a margin. Example: issued March 10 → aim to complete the purchase by August 10.

The exact last day (for example, whether the final day counts) can be confusing. Don't aim for the last day — leave a margin, and if unsure, ask NHIS at 1577-1000 or the foreign-language line 033-811-2000.

Why leave a month of margin?

Some steps take longer than people expect:

  • A consultation to check your hearing and daily life and to choose a style
  • Production time, if the hearing aid is custom-made from an ear impression (e.g., in-the-ear types)
  • Adjustments to fine-tune the sound after you start wearing it
  • Time to discuss with your family

That is why we always ask for the prescription issue date when you call to book. If there is little time left, we schedule your consultation first.

More dates after the purchase

  • After 1 month of use: inspection confirmation by sound-field test from the prescribing specialist. The benefit depends on the result.
  • Every year from 1 year after purchase: the benefit can be paid for follow-up fitting care (후기적합관리) received at least once a year.
  • 5-year service life: the NHIS table lists 5 years for hearing aids — the reference period before the same item can be covered again. Ask NHIS how it applies to you.

All in one view:

  1. Prescription issue date
  2. (within 6 months) Purchase from an NHIS-registered supplier
  3. (after 1 month of use) Inspection by the prescribing specialist
  4. Benefit claim
  5. (every year from 1 year after purchase) Follow-up fitting care

Frequently asked questions

Q. What if the 6 months have already passed?

A. Under NHIS rules, only hearing aids bought within 6 months of the prescription issue date are covered. If the period has passed, first ask NHIS (1577-1000, foreign-language line 033-811-2000) or the clinic that wrote the prescription what to do.

Q. I lost my prescription.

A. The prescription is one of the claim documents. Ask the clinic that issued it whether it can be reissued.

If the dates are confusing, book a visit and bring your prescription to our Jinhae Center (Hi-Zone Bldg., Jaeun-dong) or Myeongji Service Center. We will start by checking how much time you have left. For the full process, see the 5 steps of the hearing aid subsidy.
