Annual Follow-up Fitting for Hearing Aids: Why and How, from 1 Year After Purchase

Guiin Hearing · Written 2026-10-23 · Program details based on the NHIS website, October 2026

Key points
  • NHIS rule: from 1 year after purchase, the benefit can be paid for follow-up fitting care (후기적합관리) received at least once a year.
  • Who: people who bought their hearing aid with the National Health Insurance benefit (check eligibility, amount and frequency with NHIS).
  • At the visit: hearing re-test, check of the usage log (data log), re-adjustment, cleaning and parts check.
  • Claim documents: follow-up fitting claim form, tax invoice or card receipt, hearing test results with data-log record (fitting report).

Many people who bought a hearing aid with the National Health Insurance benefit have never heard of follow-up fitting care (후기적합관리). The Korean name sounds difficult, but simply put, it means "re-fitting your hearing aid to your ears every year."

Here is what it is, when to get it, and what to prepare. If you live a little farther away, for example in Yongwon or Ungdong, booking the date in advance makes it easy.

The NHIS rule

The National Health Insurance Service (NHIS, 국민건강보험공단) hearing aid criteria state:

"From 1 year after the purchase of a hearing aid, the benefit can be paid if follow-up fitting care is received at least once a year." (보청기 구입 후 1년이 지난 때부터 매년 1회 이상 후기적합관리를 받은 경우 급여비 지급 가능)

  • From when: once 1 year has passed since purchase
  • How often: at least once a year
  • Who: people who bought their hearing aid with the National Health Insurance benefit (check with NHIS whether this applies to you)

For the amount paid, the number of times and other details, ask NHIS at 1577-1000 or the foreign-language line 033-811-2000.

Why re-fit every year?

Your hearing aid was adjusted to your hearing and lifestyle on the day it was fitted. Over time, things change:

  • Your hearing may change little by little.
  • Your life changes — you stop working, go to more gatherings, or talk on the phone more with family.
  • Parts wear out. Filters, domes and tubes get blocked or stiff with use.

So once a year it is worth re-checking your hearing, looking at how you used the hearing aid, and adjusting it to your life now.

What we do at a follow-up fitting (at Guiin Hearing)

1. Hearing re-test

We check whether your hearing has changed since last time. If it has dropped a lot, or only one side has become worse, we will recommend seeing an ENT doctor before re-adjusting.

2. Usage log check

Hearing aids keep a record (data log) of how many hours a day they were used and in what environments. We look at it with you.

3. Re-adjustment

We ask about situations that were difficult and re-tune the sound based on the test results and the usage log.

4. Cleaning and parts check

We check and clean parts that wear out, such as filters, domes and tubes.

The NHIS claim documents also include "hearing test results and data-log record (fitting report)", which is why testing and the log check are done together.

Claim documents for follow-up fitting (NHIS)

  • Hearing aid follow-up fitting benefit claim form (보청기 적합관리 급여 청구서), 1 copy
  • Tax invoice, or credit/debit card receipt, 1 copy
  • Hearing test results and data-log record (fitting report · 피팅리포트)

The forms can be downloaded from the NHIS page "Payment criteria by assistive device > Hearing aids" (Korean). We check the documents with you when you come for your follow-up fitting.

How not to miss the date

  • Save your purchase date in your phone calendar as a yearly repeating event.
  • Book by phone in advance for a date on or after the day 1 year has passed since purchase.
  • If the hearing aid is for a parent, add the date to your own calendar as well.
  • Purchase date: ____ / __ / __
  • First follow-up fitting (from 1 year after purchase): ____ / __
  • Follow-up fitting this year: ____ / __ / __

What to bring

  • The hearing aids you are using (both)
  • The charger or batteries
  • Notes on difficult situations during the year (e.g., "hard to follow speech in restaurants", "echo on phone calls")

From Yongwon or Ungdong, you can book whichever is more convenient: the Jinhae Center (Hi-Zone Bldg., 999 Jinhae-daero, underground parking at the Hi-Zone shopping arcade) or the Myeongji Service Center in Busan.
