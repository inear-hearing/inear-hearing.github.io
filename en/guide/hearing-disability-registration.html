Registering a hearing disability in Korea: steps from application to welfare card and hearing aid benefit

Guiin Hearing · Written 2026-10-24 · Based on National Pension Service, Ministry of Health and Welfare and Korea Law Information Center sources (accessed 24 Oct 2026)

Key points
  • Apply at the community service center (읍·면·동 행정복지센터) for your registered address. You apply in person in principle; a guardian may apply for under-18s or people who cannot move easily.
  • Get a disability diagnosis at a medical institution and submit the certificate with test results. The National Pension Service (NPS, 국민연금공단) assesses the disability severity — usually within 30 days, up to 60 days for in-depth assessments.
  • The result comes back through the community center; once registered you receive a disability registration card (welfare card, 복지카드).
  • After registration, a hearing aid prescribed by an ENT specialist and bought from an NHIS-registered supplier can be covered by the NHIS assistive-device benefit.

The National Health Insurance hearing aid benefit is for people registered with a hearing disability, so registration is the first step. Here are the steps as described in official sources.

What are the steps to register a hearing disability and get hearing aid support?

  1. ① Apply for disability registration at your community service center
  2. ② Receive a diagnosis referral form (you may also visit a medical institution first)
  3. ③ Disability diagnosis at a medical institution → submit the certificate and test results
  4. ④ Disability severity assessment by the National Pension Service
  5. ⑤ Result notified → registration → disability registration card (welfare card)
  6. ⑥ ENT hearing aid prescription → purchase from an NHIS-registered supplier → NHIS benefit

Where and who applies?

  • Where: the community service center for your registered address.
  • Who: the applicant in principle; a guardian may apply for under-18s or people who cannot move easily.
  • For foreign residents, eligibility depends on visa status — check with the community center first.

How is the diagnosis done?

The diagnosis is made at a medical institution. The criteria and test methods are set out in the Ministry of Health and Welfare’s disability severity assessment standards. Follow the medical institution’s guidance on which tests are needed and how many times. Submit the diagnosis certificate together with test results and medical records; missing documents can lead to a request for more information.

How long does the NPS disability severity assessment take?

  • Usually 30 days from when the NPS branch receives the request; in-depth assessments within 60 days (one extension of up to 30 days is possible).
  • If asked for additional documents, submit them within 21 days (excluding public holidays), or the assessment may be delayed or returned.
  • If documents alone are not enough, an NPS advisory doctor may examine you directly. Time for additional documents or direct examination is not counted in the assessment period.
  • Check progress on the NPS website or call 1355.

After registration, how do I get the hearing aid benefit?

Get a prescription from an ENT specialist and buy the hearing aid from an NHIS-registered supplier within 6 months of the prescription date. After 1 month of wearing it, get the inspection, then claim the benefit. The standard amount for a hearing aid is KRW 1,310,000 (5-year service life), and NHIS pays 90% of the standard amount or the actual price, whichever is lower (100% for certain near-poverty groups). The standard amount is not a sales price. Confirm your own amount with NHIS (1577-1000; foreign-language line 033-811-2000).

See 5 steps to the hearing aid subsidy and the 6-month prescription deadline.

Q. Where do I apply for hearing disability registration?

A. At the community service center (읍·면·동 행정복지센터) for your registered address. You apply in person in principle; a guardian may apply for under-18s or people who cannot move easily.

Q. How long does the disability severity assessment take?

A. According to the National Pension Service, usually 30 days from when its branch receives the request, and within 60 days for in-depth assessments. Time for additional documents (due within 21 days) or direct examination is not counted.
