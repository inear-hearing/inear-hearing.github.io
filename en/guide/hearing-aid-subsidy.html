Korea's Hearing Aid Subsidy: 5 Steps and the Documents You Need (2026)

Guiin Hearing · Written 2026-10-23 · Program details based on the NHIS website, October 2026

Key points
  • Who: people registered with a hearing disability who are enrolled in National Health Insurance (as a subscriber or dependent), when an ENT specialist judges a hearing aid helpful for daily life.
  • How much: the standard amount is KRW 1,310,000 per 5-year service life. NHIS pays 90% of the standard amount or the actual price, whichever is lower (100% for certain near-poverty, 차상위, groups).
  • Order: confirm registration → ENT prescription → buy from an NHIS-registered supplier within 6 months of the prescription date → inspection after 1 month of use → claim.
  • Six claim documents: claim form, prescription, inspection confirmation, tax invoice or card receipt, photo showing the barcode, standard purchase contract.

The question we hear most often is "How do I get the hearing aid subsidy?" The paperwork has unfamiliar names, and you need to go between a clinic, a hearing aid center and the National Health Insurance Service (NHIS, 국민건강보험공단), so it can feel complicated at first.

This guide lays out the process in five steps, based on the rules published by NHIS. Korean terms are shown in brackets so you can show them at a community center, clinic or NHIS office.

This guide covers the National Health Insurance assistive device benefit for hearing aids (장애인보조기기 급여). Industrial accident insurance (산재보험) and Medical Aid (의료급여) have different rules.

Key numbers: KRW 1,310,000 standard amount, 5-year service life, NHIS pays 90%

  • Standard amount (기준액): KRW 1,310,000 · Service life (내구연한): 5 years — from the NHIS table of standard amounts and service life for assistive devices.
  • Amount NHIS pays (공단 부담금액): 90% of the standard amount or the actual purchase price, whichever is lower. For certain near-poverty groups eligible for reduced co-payment (차상위 본인부담경감 대상자) it is 100%.
  • How often: the same device is paid once per person within its service life (5 years for hearing aids).

The standard amount is not a sales price; it is a reference amount set by the program. NHIS pays 90% of the standard amount or the actual price, whichever is lower (100% for certain near-poverty groups).

Rules may differ for Medical Aid recipients and others. For your exact amount, call NHIS at 1577-1000 (Korean) or the NHIS foreign-language line 033-811-2000 (English, Chinese, Vietnamese, Uzbek).

Step 1. Check that you are registered with a hearing disability

The benefit is for people registered with a hearing disability (청각장애 · 청력장애). In addition, an ENT (ear, nose and throat) specialist must judge that a hearing aid will help in daily life (NHIS rule).

  • If you already have a welfare card for registered persons with disabilities (복지카드) → go to Step 2.
  • If not → you first need to register. Applications are made at the community center (주민센터) for your registered address. For details, ask the community center or check the Ministry of Health and Welfare (보건복지부) guidance.
  • Foreign nationals: whether you can register a disability depends on your residence status (visa). Please ask your community center before you start.
  • I have a welfare card (복지카드) or proof of disability registration

Step 2. Get a hearing aid prescription from an ENT specialist

Hearing aid prescriptions are written by an ENT specialist (이비인후과 전문의). You can choose any clinic or hospital that is convenient for you.

Under NHIS rules, the prescription is based on hearing tests of both ears (eardrum and ear canal condition, average pure-tone threshold, speech discrimination), and the test results are attached.

The basic benefit is for one ear. A benefit for both ears requires meeting all of NHIS's additional conditions, such as age and hearing criteria. This is checked at the prescription stage.

  • I have the assistive device prescription (보조기기 처방전)
  • I wrote the prescription issue date on my calendar (you need it in Step 3)

Step 3. Buy from an NHIS-registered supplier within 6 months of the prescription date

This is where many people miss out.

  • Only devices bought within 6 months of the prescription issue date are covered.
  • The hearing aid must be bought from a supplier registered with NHIS (공단 등록 업소).
  • When you buy, you sign a standard hearing aid purchase contract (보청기 구매 표준계약서). It is one of the claim documents.

When you book, tell us the prescription issue date and we will plan your consultation and fitting around the time you have left. See also how to count the 6-month deadline.

  • Less than 6 months have passed since the issue date
  • I received the standard purchase contract
  • I received a tax invoice or card receipt (with itemized statement)

Step 4. After 1 month of use, get the inspection from the prescribing specialist

It does not end when you receive the hearing aid. NHIS requires an inspection confirmation through a sound-field test after at least 1 month of wearing the hearing aid, done by the same specialist who wrote the prescription. The benefit is paid when the inspection finds that the hearing aid improves your hearing (NHIS wording).

So it is important to wear the hearing aid regularly during the first month. If something is uncomfortable, don't put up with it — come in for an adjustment during that time.

  • At least 1 month has passed since I started wearing it
  • I received the inspection confirmation (보조기기 검수확인서)

Step 5. Claim the benefit — document checklist

These are the claim documents listed by NHIS:

  • Assistive device benefit claim form (보조기기급여 지급청구서), 1 copy
  • Assistive device prescription (보조기기 처방전), 1 copy
  • Inspection confirmation (보조기기 검수확인서), 1 copy
  • Tax invoice, or credit/debit card receipt (세금계산서 또는 카드 전표), 1 copy — card receipts with an itemized statement
  • Photo of the device showing its barcode (바코드 사진), 1 copy
  • Standard hearing aid purchase contract (보청기 구매 표준계약서), 1 copy

The forms can be downloaded from the NHIS page "Payment criteria by assistive device > Hearing aids" (Korean). After your purchase, Guiin Hearing goes through the documents with you one by one.

After the purchase: annual follow-up fitting

From 1 year after purchase, the benefit can also be paid for follow-up fitting care (후기적합관리) received at least once a year (NHIS rule). Plan a check-up date each year, on or after the day 1 year has passed since purchase. See annual follow-up fitting.

What to bring to your consultation

  • Welfare card (복지카드)
  • Assistive device prescription (check the issue date)
  • Hearing test results
  • Your current hearing aid, if you have one
