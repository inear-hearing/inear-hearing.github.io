Government subsidy for hearing aids

If you are registered with a hearing disability and enrolled in Korea's National Health Insurance (as a subscriber or dependent), you can receive the assistive-device benefit when you buy a hearing aid from a supplier registered with the National Health Insurance Service (NHIS). The standard amount for a hearing aid is ₩1,310,000 (5-year service life), and NHIS pays 90% of the standard amount or the actual price, whichever is lower (100% for certain near-poverty groups, 차상위). NHIS rule (Korean) · 5 steps to get the subsidy

For your exact amount, contact NHIS at 1577-1000 (foreign-language line 033-811-2000).

National Health Insurance hearing-aid prescriptions are issued by an ENT specialist of your own choice. After the prescription, hearing aids can be purchased from a provider registered with the National Health Insurance Service, and Guiin Hearing guides you through the purchase, inspection and benefit-claim paperwork from start to finish.

Hearing-disability registration process

  1. Visit an ENT clinic for examination
  2. Pure-tone audiometry ×3 + ABR (auditory brainstem response) test ×1
  3. Test results & disability diagnosis certificate issued
  4. Apply for disability registration at your local community center
  5. Disability severity review by the National Pension Service
  6. Welfare card issued
  7. Hearing-aid prescription & assistive-device subsidy application

Frequently Asked Questions

How much government subsidy can I get for a hearing aid?
If you are registered with a hearing disability and enrolled in Korea's National Health Insurance, you can receive the assistive-device benefit when you buy a hearing aid from an NHIS-registered supplier. The standard amount is ₩1,310,000 (5-year service life), and NHIS pays 90% of the standard amount or the actual price, whichever is lower (100% for certain near-poverty groups). The same device is covered once per person within its service life. For your exact amount, contact NHIS (1577-1000; foreign-language line 033-811-2000). National Health Insurance hearing-aid prescriptions are issued by an ENT specialist of your own choice. After the prescription, hearing aids can be purchased from a provider registered with the National Health Insurance Service, and Guiin Hearing guides you through the purchase, inspection and benefit-claim paperwork from start to finish. See the 5 steps to get the hearing aid subsidy.

How do I register a hearing disability?
Apply at the community service center for your registered address, get a disability diagnosis at a medical institution and submit the certificate and test results; the National Pension Service then reviews the disability severity (usually 30 days, up to 60 for in-depth reviews). Once registered, you receive a welfare card. Hearing disability registration steps →
What are the steps to the hearing aid subsidy?
Hearing disability registration, ENT prescription, purchase from an NHIS-registered supplier within 6 months of the prescription date, inspection by the prescribing specialist after 1 month of wear, then the benefit claim. From one year after purchase, the annual follow-up fitting can also be covered. 5 steps to the subsidy →
Can I buy a hearing aid before getting a prescription and still get the subsidy?
The NHIS hearing aid benefit covers devices bought from an NHIS-registered supplier within 6 months of an ENT specialist’s prescription date. Buying first may mean no benefit, so get the prescription first. Confirm with NHIS (1577-1000). The 6-month prescription deadline →
Can noise-induced hearing loss be covered by workers’ compensation?
According to COMWEL, noise-induced hearing loss meeting the criteria (3+ years of exposure to continuous noise of 85 dB(A)+, 40 dB+ hearing loss in one ear, and other conditions) can receive disability benefits and hearing aid support. COMWEL (1588-0075) decides each case. Workers’ compensation and hearing aids →
What makes hearing aid prices differ?
Style (behind-the-ear or in-the-ear), sound processing features, rechargeable or battery, one or both ears, included items and aftercare, and whether the NHIS benefit applies. We give exact prices at the consultation. 6 price factors →
How do I get my hearing aid repaired or checked?
Call 010-6599-0109 to book and bring your hearing aid with its charger or batteries to the Jinhae or Myeongji center. If it sounds quiet, first check power, earwax and filters, moisture and settings. If hearing in one ear drops suddenly or dizziness or tinnitus appears, do not delay — see an ENT doctor as soon as possible. 5 checks at home →
